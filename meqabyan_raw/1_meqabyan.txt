
=== Chapter 1 ===

1
  A: A certain man named ᵇ Tseerutsaydan loved his sin. He would often boast of the great profusion of his troops and of the great armies which he held governance over.
  B: ᵃ There were one man whose name are called ᵇ Tseerutsaydan an who love sin ~ him would boast ina him horses abundance an him troops firmness beneath him authority.

2
  A: He had countless priests who served the false gods which he worshiped, and [he would often bow to them and make sacrifices by night and day.
  B: Him had many priests who serve him idols whom him worship an fe whom him bow an sacrifice sacrifice by night an by daylight.

3
  A: In the sightlessness of his own heart, he believed that they granted him power and strength.
  B: But ina him heart dullness it would seem fe him that them give him firmness an Power.

4
  A: He believed that they gave him sovereignty over the nations.
  B: An ina him heart it would seem fe him that them give him authority ina all him Rule.

5
  A: And as his sway over the nations progressed, he believed that [these false gods] granted him the right to supremacy.
  B: An again ina formation time it would seem fe him that them give him all the desired authority also.

6
  A: [The King] would offer sacrifices to them by night and day,
  B: An him would sacrifice sacrifice fe them day an night.

7
  A: appointing priests to serve these idols.
  B: Him appointed priests who serve him idols.

8
  A: These priests ᵃ ate from the unclean sacrifices, all the while telling the king that the idols had consumed what was offered.
  B: While them ᵃ ate from that defouled sacrifice—them would tell him pretendin that the idols eat night an day.

9
  A: They sought to ᵃ proselytize among the people, so that they might make greater sacrifices to be consumed.
  B: Again them would ᵃmek other persons diligent like unto them—that them might sacrifice sacrifice an eat. An again them would mek other persons diligent that them might sacrifice sacrifice—an sacrifice sacrifice like unto them.

10
  A: The king trusted in these idols, which can grant no favors, nor yield any merit to his welfare.
  B: But him would trust ina him idols that don't profit nor benefit.

11
  A: At the time, when his heart was blind to the truth, [the king] believed that they had given him to fate, by placing him with a diadem. It would appear to him that [these idols] had created him, for ᵃ Satan blocked him from knowledge of the true God, who alone grants eternal life. Rather, [Satan] sought to bring him away from life, into the fire of ᵇ Gehenna forever, for he believed the idols to be gods,
  B: By him timeframe bein small—an ina him heart dullness—it would seem fe him that them Irated him—that them feed him an that them crown him ~ it would seem fe him that them Irated him—fe ᵃ Seythan have deafened him reasonin lest him know him Irator Who Irated him bringin from not livin toward livin—or lest him with him kindreds know him Irator Who Irated him bringin from not livin toward livin—that them might go toward ᵇ Gehennem of Fiyah foriva—it bein judged pon them with him who call them gods without them bein gods.

12
  A: but since they do not truly live, they cannot give life.
  B: As them aren't never well whenever—it are due that him might call them dead ones.

13
  A: It was Satan’s tyranny that mislead them, which was found within their idolatry. He gave of them false speech, and said he would reveal great truths to them, and for this he was loved. [Therefore, Satan] cast the people’s discernment upon the false gods and those that believe in them, and among all the children of Adam, who’s prayers had become like that of the dust.
  B: As Seythan authority that mislead them will lodge ina that idol image—an as him will tell them them reasonin accord—an as him will reveal fe them like unto them loved—him will judge pon the idols wherein them believed an wherein 'Adam childran trust—whose reasonin were like unto ashes.

14
  A: They gazed upon [the idols] in solemn reverence, and [Satan] filled their mind with their own desires. And the people complied, offering as sacrifice their young girls and young boys, children of their own blood. They spilled the innocent blood of their children.
  B: An them will marvel pon the time them sight up that him fulfilled what them thought fe them—an them will do him accord fe him reachin up til them sacrifice them dawta childran an them male childran birthed from them nature—up til them spill them dawta childran an male childran blood that were clean.

15
  A: This did not concern [the king], for Satan had savored the sacrifices he made to his gods in order to complete his malicious designs. [Satan ᵃ sought to] bring him down into Gehenna, where he dwells, and where there is no respite for all eternity, and where all will receive chastisement.
  B: Them didn't sadden them—fe Seythan have savoured him sacrifice fe them fe fulfill them evil accord—that him might lower them toward Gehannem ᵃ like unto him—where there are no exits up til Iternity—where him will raceive tribulation.

16
  A: This king, Tseerutsaydan, was full of conceit; for he had fifty idols made of false gods, and twenty idols of false goddesses.
  B: But that Tseerutsaydan were arrogant ~ him had fifty idols worked ina males pattern an twenty worked ina dawtaz pattern.

17
  A: He would often boast about his idolatry, and offered glorification and praise to the idols while he made his daily sacrifices.
  B: An him would boast ina those idols that have no benefit ~ him would totally glorify them while him sacrificed sacrifice mornin an evenin.

18
  A: He commanded the people, that they too might make sacrifices to these idols. [The king] would eat from these unclean sacrifices, and ᵃ ordered that the people do the same, to spread the evil which he created.
  B: An him would command persons that them might sacrifice sacrifice fe the idols—an him would eat from that defouled sacrifice—an him would ᵃ command other persons that them might eat from the sacrifice ~ him would especially provoke fe evil.

19
  A: Now, [the king] had five workshops which were under his command, where he forged the idols out of iron, brass and lead.
  B: Him had five houses worked fe him beaten worked idols that were iron an brass an lead.

20
  A: The king had then adorned in silver and in gold, and had veiled curtains around his palaces for them, each hidden in a tabernacle.
  B: An him ornamanted them ina silver an gold ~ him veiled curtains around the houses fe them an planted a tent fe them.

21
  A: He appointed guardians for their care, and would make offerings of meat to these idols from forty creatures—ten fattened oxen and ten fatted ewes, ten barren calves and ten barren goats, along with winged birds of the air.
  B: Him appointed keepers fe them there ~ him would Itinually sacrifice forty fe him idols—ten fattened oxen—ten sterile cows—ten fattened sheep ewes—ten barren goats—with birds that have wings.

22
  A: It appeared to [the king] that these idols ate and drank, consuming fifty bunches of grapes and fifty dishes of bread kneaded with oil.
  B: —But it would seem fe him that him idols ate ~ him would present fe them fifty feeqen of grapes an fifty dishes of wheat kneaded with oil.

23
  A: The king said to the priests, “Take this, give it to them. Let my gods consume the meat that has been slaughtered on their account. Make them drink from of the wine I have given to them. If this is not enough to quench their desire, I will give them more.”
  B: An him told him priests ~ :"Tek an give them—mek mi irators eat what mi slaughtered fe them—an mek them drink the grape mi presented fe them—as fe if it aren't enough fe them—mi will add fe them."

24
  A: [The king] then ordered that everyone should eat and drink from that defiling sacrifice.
  B: An him would command all that them might eat an drink from that defouled sacrifice.

25
  A: He then, in his corruption, sent out his armies to march on all the nations of his realm, to find one who would neither make sacrifices, nor ᵃ prostrate themselves [before the idols], so that they could be brought before him. He sought to punish them by ᵇ fire and by sword, [for he feared that the gods] might seize his wealth and burn his palaces. [The king] lived in ᶜ terror that they might remove his great fortune and destroy him.
  B: But ina him evil malice him would send him troops who visit ina all the kingdom—that as it were there were one who neither sacrifice nor ᵃ bow —them might separate an know an bring him—an might punish him by ᵇ fiyah an by sword before him—that them might plunder him money an might burn him house ina fiyah—that them might downstroy all him money him had pon him.

26
  A: “My gods are generous and great, and out of their benevolence I have forged them. Therefore, I will give chastisement on him that does not worship my gods and make sacrifices to them.
  B: "Fe them are kind an great ones—an fe them have Irated wi ina them charity—an mi will show punishmant an tribulation fe him unless him worshipped mi irators an sacrificed sacrifice fe mi irators.

27
  A: I will show punish punishment and retribution! For they have created the heavens and the earth, the great wide sea, the moon and sun, the rains and the wind, and all that abide in this world.
  B: An mi will show him punishmant an tribulation—fe them have Irated Earth an Heaven an the sea that were wide an moon an Sun an stars an rains an winds an all that live ina this world fe be food an fe be satiety fe wi."

28
  A: Those who will not worship shall be punished under the penalty of law, and I shall have no mercy.
  B: But persons who worship them shall be punished ina firm tribulation—an them won't be nice fe them.

  A: DPC: "This name is a curiosity, as ordinarily the Maccabee revolt is associated with the Seleucid king, Antiochus IV Epiphanius, who is otherwise unmentioned in this text. The meaning of this name is a subject of debate. It has been suggested that this name appears to represent the folk memory of the aforementioned tyrant. Some coinage from his reign give his dual capital cities, and are noted with the inscription “Tyre and Sidon”, which appears to constitute the transliteration of this name into Ge’ez, and later Amharic. This would also account for the pairing of these two Punic cities later in the text. Alternatively, since the Ethiopian Synaxarion calls that nation of Syria ‘Saranin’ (Tahisas 29), This name could also be a cognate for the ‘King of Syria’."

=== Chapter 2 ===

1
  A: ᵃ There were one man birthed from the tribe of Binyam whose name are called Meqabees;
  B: ᵃ There was a man born of the tribe of Benjamin who is called Maccabeus;

2
  A: him had three childran who were handsome an totally warriors ~ them had bein iloved alongside all persons ina that Midyam an Miedon country that are Tseerutsaydan Rule.
  B: He had three children who were handsome and were true warriors—they were beloved by all people in Midian and Media under Tseerutsaydan's rule.

3
  A: An like unto the king commanded them pon the time him found them:—"Don't unu bow fe Tseerutsaydan irators? How about don't unu sacrifice sacrifice?
  B: And the king commanded them at the time he found them: "Do you not bow to Tseerutsaydan 's creators? Why do you not sacrifice unto them?

4
  A: But if unu refuse—wi will seize an tek unu toward the king—an wi will downstroy all your money like unto the king commanded."
  B: If you refuse, we will seize you and bring you before the king, and we will destroy all your money as the king commanded.

5
  A: These youts who were handsome replied fe him sayin—"As fe Him fe Whom InI bow—there are InI Faada Irator Who Irated Earth an Heaven an what are within she—an the sea—moon an Sun an clouds an stars ~ Him are the True Irator Whom InI worship an ina Whom InI believe."
  B: These handsome youths replied to him saying, "As for Him to Whom we bow, Our Father Creator Who Created Earth and Heaven and all that is in her, and the sea, moon and Sun and clouds and stars—He is the True Creator Whom we worship and in Whom we believe."

6
  A: An these the king youts are four—an them servants who carry shield an spear are a hundred.
  B: And these the king's youths are four, and their servants who carry shield and spear are a hundred.

7
  A: An pon the time them loved that them might seize these hola ones—them escaped from them hands and there are none who touched them ~ as those youts are totally warriors ina Power—them went seizin shields an them spears.
  B: And at that time they desired that they might seize these holy ones, they escaped from their hands and there were none that touched them—as those youths were true warriors in the Power, they seized their shields and their spears.

8
  A: An there were from them one who strangle an kill panther—an at that time him would strangle it like unto a chicken.
  B: And there was one among them who could strangle and kill a panther, and at that time he would strangle it like unto a chicken.

9
  A: An there were one from them who kill a lion with one rock or strikin at one time with a stick.
  B: And there was one among them who could kill a lion with one stone or one strike with a stick

10
  A: An there were one from them who kill a hundred persons—strikin ina formation time with one sword—an them name an them hunt were thus ~ it were called ina all Babilon an Mo`ab countries.
  B: And there was one among them who could kill a hundred men, striking in formation with one sword, and their name and manner of hunting—it was thus called in all of Babylon and Moab.

11
  A: An them were warriors ina Power—an them had a thing bein iloved an comeliness.
  B: And they were warriors in the Power, and they were beloved and comely.

12
  A: An again them features comeliness were wondrous—however becau them worshipped JAH an becau them didn't fear death—it are them reasonin comeliness that surpass all.
  B: And again their comely features were wonderous—However because they worshiped Jehovah and because they did not fear death, it is the comeliness of their hearts that surpassed all.

13
  A: An pon the time them frightened the troops—there are none who could able fe seize them—but them who were warriors escaped proceedin toward a lofty mountain.
  B: And at that time they frightened the troops, there were none who were able to seize them, but they who were warriors escaped to a lofty mountain

14
  A: An those troops returned toward the city an shut the fortress gate ~ them terrorized the people sayin—"Unless unu brought those warriors the Meqabyans—wi will burn your city ina fiyah—an wi will send toward the king an downstroy your country."
  B: And these troops returned to the city and shut the fortress gate—they terrorized the people saying, "Unless you bring forth those warriors the Maccabees, we will burn your city with fire, and we will send for the king and destroy your country.

15
  A: An at that time the country persons—rich an poor ones an dawtaz an males—a child whose faada an mother dead pon him an old dawtaz—everyone proceeded an shouted together—an them straightened them necks toward the mountain an shouted toward them sayin—"Don't downstroy InI—an don't downstroy InI country pon InI."
  B: And at that time the people of that country, rich and poor, women and men, orphans and and old women, everyone turned their necks toward the mountains and shouted toward them saying, "Do not destroy us, and do not destroy our country upon us."

16
  A: At that time them wept together—an them feared—arisin from JAH.
  B: At that time they wept together, and the fear of Jehovah arose.

17
  A: Returnin them faces Eastward an streachin forth them hands them begged toward JAH together—"Lord—should InI refuse these men who demolished Thy Command an Thy LAW?
  B: Turning their faces Eastward and stretching forth their hands, together they begged Jehovah, "Lord, should we refuse these men who demolished Thy Command and thy Law?

18
  A: Yet him believed ina silver an gold an ina the stone an wood that a person hands worked—but InI don't love that InI might hear that criminal word—who didn't believe Thy LAW" them said.
  B: Yet he believed in silver and gold and in stone and wood that a persons hands have worked, but we do not love that we might hear that criminal word, who did not believe thy Law."

19
  A: "When Thou are the Irator Who save an Who kill—him mek him ras self like unto them Irated him also ~ as fe him—him are who spill a person blood an who eat a person flesh.
  B: Whereas thou art the Creator Who saves and Who kills, he maketh himself lord like unto them who created him also, he spilleth man's blood and eateth a man's flesh.

20
  A: But InI don't love that InI might sight up that criminal face nor hear him word" them said.
  B: "But we do not love that we might see his lawless face nor hear his words.

21
  A: "However if Thou commanded InI—InI will go toward him ~ becau InI believe ina Thee-I—InI will pass an give InI bodies fe death—an pon the time him said 'Sacrifice sacrifice fe mi irators'—InI won't hear that criminal word.
  B: But if thou commandeth us, we will go to him—because we believe in Thee, we will go and give up our bodies for death, and at that time he sayeth, 'Make sacrifices unto my creators', we will not hear that lawless word.

22
  A: But InI believed Thee-I—Lord Who examine kidneys an reasonins—InI Faadas Irator—'Abriham an Yis'haq an Ya`iqob who did Thy Accord an lived firmed up ina Thy LAW.
  B: But we believe in Thee, Who knowest the kidneys and hearts, in the Creator of our Fathers, Abraham and Issac and Jacob who did Thy Accord and lived steadfastly in Thy Law.

23
  A: Thou examine a person reasonin an help the sinner an the righteous one—an there be none hidden from Thee-I—an him who took refuge are revealed alongside Thee-I.
  B: Thou knowest a man's heart and helpest the sinner and the righteous, and there is nothing hidden from Thee, and he who takes refuge is revealed to the.

24
  A: But InI have no other Irator apart from Thee-I.
  B: But wee have no other Creator than Thee.

25
  A: That InI might give InI bodies fe death becau Thy glorified Name—however be Power an Firmness an a Shelter fe InI ina this Work that InI are ruled fe Thee-I.
  B: That we might give up our bodies to death because of Thy glorified Name, but be thou Power and Firmness and a Shelter for us in this Work that we are ruled by Thee.

26
  A: An pon the time 'Isra'iel entered toward Gibts country Thou heard Ya`iqob plea—an now glorified God—InI beg Thee-I."
  B: And at the time Israel entered into Egypt Thou heard Jacob's plea. And now, glorified God, we beseech Thee."

27
  A: An pon the time the two men whose features were quite handsome were sight up fe them standin before them—pon the time fiyah swords that frighten like unto lightnin alit an cut them necks an killed them—at that time them arose bein well like unto formerly.
  B: And at that time the two handsome men appeared before them, and with frightening firey swords that appeared like lightning they cut their necks and killed them

28
  A: Them features comeliness became totally handsome an them shone more than Sun—an them became more handsome than formerly.
  B: The comeliness of their features became very handsome and shone brighter than the Sun, and they became more handsome than before.

=== Chapter 3 ===

1
  A: Like unto unu sight up before unu these the Most I JAH slaves—'Abya—Seela—Fentos who dead an arose—unu have that unu might arise likewise after unu dead—an your faces shall shine like unto the Sun ina the Kingdom of Heaven.
  B: As thou seest before thee, the servants of the Most High Jehovah, Abijah, Seelah, Fentos who were dead and arose, you have that you might likewise arise from the dead, and your faces shall shine like unto the Sun in the Kingdom of Heaven.

2
  A: An them went with those men an raceived martyrdom there.
  B: And they went with those men and received martyrdom there.

3
  A: At that time them begged—them praised—an them bowed fe JAH ~ death didn't frighten them an the king punishmant didn't frighten them.
  B: And at that time they begged, they praised, and they bowed before Jehovah—death did not frighten them and the king's punishment did not frighten them.

4
  A: An them went toward those youts an became like unto a sheep that have no evil—yet them didn't frighten them—an pon the time them arrived toward them—them seized an beat them an bound an whipped them—an them delivered them toward the king an stood them before him.
  B: And they went toward those youths and became like unto a sheep that has no evil, yet they did not frighten them, and upon the time that they arrived, they seized and beat them and bound and whipped them, and they delivered them up to the king and placed them before him.

5
  A: An the king answered fe them sayin—"How won't unu stubborn ones sacrifice sacrifice an bow fe mi irators?"
  B: And the king answered them saying, "Why will ye stubborn ones not sacrifice sacrifices and bow before my creators?"

6
  A: Those bredren who were cleansed from sin—who were honoured an chosen an Irie—an who shine like unto a jewel whose value were wondrous—Seela an 'Abya an Fentos answered fe him ina one word.
  B: Those brethren who were cleansed from sin, who were honored and chosen and joyful, and who shined like a jewel of wondrous value, Seelah and Abijah and Fentos answered him in one word.

7
  A: Them told that king who were a plague—"As fe InI—InI won't bow nor sacrifice fe defouled idols that have no knowledge nor reasonin."
  B: They told that king who was a plague, "As for us, we will not bow nor sacrifice unto defouled idols that have not knowledge nor heart."

8
  A: An again them told him—"InI won't bow fe idols that were silver an gold that a person hand worked—that were stone an wood—that have no reasonin nor soul nor knowledge—that don't benefit them friends nor harm them enemies."
  B: And again, they told him, "We will not bow before idols of silver and gold that a man has wrought with his hands, of stone and wood, that have no heart nor soul nor knowledge, that do not benefit their friends nor harm their enemies."

9
  A: An the king answered fe them sayin—"Why do unu do thus—an as them know who insult them an who wrong them—why do unu insult the glorified irators?"
  B: And the king answered them, saying, "Why doest thou thus, as they know who insults them and who wrongs them? Why do you insult the glorified creators?

10
  A: Them answered fe him sayin—"As them are like unto a trifle alongside InI—as fe InI—InI will insult them an won't glorify them."
  B: They answered him, saying, "They are like unto a trifle alongside us; as for us, we will insult them and will not glorify them.

11
  A: An the king answered fe them sayin—"Mi will punish unu like unto your Work evil measure ~ mi will downstroy your features comeliness with whippin an firm tribulation an fiyah.
  B: And the king answered them, saying, "I will punish thee with an evil measure according to your works; I will destroy your comeliness with whipping and firm tribulation and fire.

12
  A: An now tell mi whether unu will give or won't give sacrifice fe mi irators—as fe if this didn't happen—mi will punish unu by sword an by whippin."
  B: And now tell me whether ye will give or not give sacrifice unto my creators, and if ye do not this, I will punish you by the sword and by whipping."

13
  A: Them answered fe him sayin—"As fe InI—InI won't sacrifice sacrifice nor bow fe defouled idols"—an the king commanded them that them might beat them with a fat stick—an again that them might whip them with a whip—an after it—that them might splinter them up til them inner organs were sight up.
  B: They answered him, saying, "As for us, we will not sacrifice nor bow unto defouled idols." And the king commanded them that they might beat them with a fat stick, and again, that they might whip them with a whip, and after that, that they might tear their flesh until their innards might be seen.

14
  A: An after this them bound an made them while ina jail house up til him counsel by money that punish an kill them.
  B: And after this they bound them and kept them in prison until he counseled with money that he might punish and kill them.

15
  A: Without niceness them took an bound them a firm imprisonmant ina prison house—an them sat ina prison house three nights an three daylights.
  B: Without mercy they took them and bound them securely in prison, and they sat in prison for three nights and three days.

16
  A: An after this third day the king commanded that a Proclamation speaker might turn an that counselors an nobles—country elders an officials—might be gathered.
  B: And after the third day the king commanded that a proclamation speaker might turn, and that counselors and nobles, local elders and officials, might be gathered.

17
  A: An pon the time the king Tseerutsaydan sat ina square—him commanded that them might bring those honoured ones—Seela an 'Abya an Fentos ~ them stood before him bein wounded an bound.
  B: And at that time Tseerutsaydan sat in the square; he commanded that they might bring forth those honored ones, Seelah and Abijah and Fentos; they stood before him, wounded and bound.

18
  A: An the king told them—"When unu sat these three days—are there really the returnin that unu returned—or are unu ina your former evil?"
  B: And the king said unto them, "When you sat (in prison) these three days, will you recall (your words), or will you remain in your former evil?"

19
  A: An those honoured JAH Souljahs answered fe him sayin—"As fe that InI were cruel—InI won't agree that InI might worship the idols filled of sin an evil that thou check up."
  B: And those honored soldiers of Jehovah answered him saying, "Even with this cruelty, we will not agree to worship the idols filled with sin that thou dost regard.

20
  A: An that criminal vexed an commanded that them might stand them up ina lofty place an might renew them wounds ~ them blood flowed pon Earth.
  B: And that man of lawlessness was vexed and commanded them to stand them up in a lofty place and renew their wounds, and their blood flowed upon the Earth.

21
  A: An again him commanded that them might burn them with a torch lamp an might char them flesh—an him servants did like unto him commanded them—an those honoured men told him—"Thou who forgot JAH LAW—speak ~ InI reward shall abound ina the measure whereby thou multiply InI punishmant."
  B: And again he commanded them that they might burn them with a torch lamp and might char their flesh, and his servants did as he commanded them. And those honored men said unto him, "Thou who didst not remember the Law of Jehovah, behold, our reward shall abound in the measure whereby thou multipliest our punishment."

22
  A: An again him commanded that them might bring an send pon them bears an tigers an lions that were evil beasts before them eat them food that them might totally eat them flesh with them bones.
  B: And again, he commanded that they might bring and send forth upon them bears and tigers and lions: evil beasts before them to consume them, that they might utterly consume their flesh with their bones.

23
  A: An him commanded persons who keep the beasts that them might send the beasts pon them—an them did like unto him commanded them—an them bound those honoured martyrs feet—an again them maliciously beat an bound them with tent-stakes.
  B: And he commanded the beastkeepers that they might send the beasts upon them, and they did according to his word, and they bound those honored martyrs' feet, and again, they maliciously beat them and bound them with tent-stakes.

24
  A: An those beasts were flung over them while them roared—an pon the time them arrived toward the martyrs them hailed an bowed fe them.
  B: And those beasts were flung over them while they roared, and when they reached the martyrs they hailed and bowed unto them.
