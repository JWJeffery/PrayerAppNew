Privacy notice: parish prayer requests

This is a plain-language description of how the parish prayer-request feature handles information. It is not legal advice.

A word before you add anything

Add names only with the person's or family's permission — first names or initials are fine. Please don't share overly sensitive information, such as detailed medical, legal, financial or family details. When in doubt, keep it general.

What is stored

  • For rectors and helpers: your email address, the name you give us, and your role.
  • Prayer requests: the text a rector or helper types, its category and its dates.
  • For rate limiting only: a scrambled (hashed) form of your network address, which cannot be turned back into the address. These are deleted within two days.
  • Records of actions: a short log that says what was done (for example, “a request was added”), never the request text, names or email addresses. These are deleted after 180 days.

What is not stored

  • Readers who follow a parish have no account. Nothing about them is stored on our servers.
  • No tracking, advertising or analytics is used on these pages.
  • Reminder emails to rectors give counts and dates only, never the text of a request.

How long requests are kept

  • A request appears for the period the rector chose (usually 21 days), then stops being shown to readers.
  • Expired requests are permanently deleted 7 days after they expire. Removing a request deletes it immediately. There is no history of past prayer text.
  • Nightly backups of the database exist so we can recover from a failure; they are kept for a short, fixed period and are treated as confidential.

Deleting your information

  • Any rector or helper can delete their own access at any time from Parish settings → Delete my membership.
  • A rector can delete the whole parish, with all its requests and helpers, from the same page.
  • Or write to admin@theuniversaloffice.com and we will do it for you.

Who can see what

A parish's requests are shown only to people who follow that parish (and, if the parish uses a join code, only to people who have entered it). Staff of one parish cannot see another parish's information. The site administrator can see which parishes exist and how many requests each has, but not the text of any request.
