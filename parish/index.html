          Who can follow your parish

          Your join code

          Give this code to the people in your parish. They enter it once in their app or browser.

          Making a new code signs everyone out of your prayer list until they enter the new one. Do this if the code has spread further than you intended.

          People who can manage the list

            Add a helper

            A helper can add, edit, extend and remove requests. Up to 5 helpers. They sign in with their own email address.

            Leaving

            Remove my own access to this parish. Your parish and its requests stay as they are.

            Delete the whole parish

            This permanently deletes your parish, every prayer request, and every helper's access. It cannot be undone.
